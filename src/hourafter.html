Hour After

risograph booklet, 2015

Hour After is a limited run booklet that was created as a 'reader' for After Hour, an installation completed in 2015 in Oakland, CA.

The Hour After Reader was created as part of After Hour, an exhibition at Interface Gallery. It included contributions from artists and writers who create their work “after hours” along with research from the project. Contributors included: Luca Antonucci + David Kasprzak, Sofía Córdova, Aurora Crispin, Ian Dolton-Thornton, ERNEST, Brett Goodroad, Pablo Guardiola, Emily Hunt, Cybele Lyle, Martin Machado, Ortega y Gasset Project, Emma Spertus, Stairwell’s, Laura Steenberge, and Cassie Thornton..
