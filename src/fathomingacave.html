Fathoming a Cave

Collaborative Performance with Laura Steenberge at Sutro Cave in San Francisco, 2014

Part of White Hot Lamp Black at Southern Exposure

Fathoming A Cave was a collaborative performance and after dark investigation into echolocation at Sutro Cave in San Francisco. Talks about history of the cave, whale falls, sonic moth testicles, the size of sound, and the historic practice of measuring depth were all accompanied by performances by Laura Sterrnberge on her upright bass.
