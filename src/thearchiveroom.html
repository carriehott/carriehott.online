The Archive Room

research residency for the Key Room at Headlands Center for the Arts in 2014

The Key Room project began with an extensive research and excavation process at the Headlands Center for the Arts. Over the course of two years, I archived the objects left behind in the “Archive Room” and learned about their history from Holly Blake, Headlands Center for the Arts Residency Manager. I organized the objects into eleven categories and made temporary displays over the course of developing the project.
