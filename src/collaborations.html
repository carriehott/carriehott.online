how to slow internet

collective project co stewarded by Andrea Steves and Timothy Furstnau; currently in development

Our Shiver

solar powered server, website, and publication with contributions by Jacob Kahn, Megan Prelinger, Chia Amisola, Andrea Steves, and Xiaowei Wang; publication designed by Chris Hamamoto, printed by Colpa Press, published by The Lab

The Light that Elongated the Day

Installation at the Museum of Capitalism in Oakland, CA, and free six-week class open to the public that collectively created a publication which included documentation of what was explored and created in the class, alongside contributions from each participant

The Key Room

Site-specific permanent installation at the Headlands Center for the Arts that included contributions from over 100 artists in the form of objects, sounds, and guides.

The Archive Room

Site-specific installation using the hundreds of objects left behind in the “Archive Room” at the Headlands Center for the Arts, as part of the Key Room project

After Hour

Installation that included a site-specific sound created with musician Laura Steenberge

Hour After

The Hour After Reader was created as part of After Hour, an exhibition at Interface Gallery. It included contributions from artists and writers who create their work “after hours” along with research from the project.

Fathoming A Cave

Collaborative performance with Laura Steenberge at Sutro Cave in San Francisco, 2014. Part of White Hot Lamp Black at Southern Exposure

JOAN5000

'Self driving' soapbox derby car made with Kate Rhoades
